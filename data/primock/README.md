# PriMock57 sample consultations

The `.mp3` files here are derived from **PriMock57**, a dataset of 57 mock primary-care
consultations, by mixing the released doctor and patient audio channels into one track.
`*_note.json` is the consulting clinician's reference note, used as ground truth for the
generated consultation note.

- Source: <https://github.com/babylonhealth/primock57>
- Paper: Papadopoulos Korfiatis, Moramarco, Sarac, Savkov. *PriMock57: A Dataset of Primary
  Care Mock Consultations.* Proceedings of ACL 2022.
- Licence: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0)**. These derived files
  are redistributed under the same licence.

The consultations are acted, not real patients. Regenerate with
`python scripts/fetch_primock.py`.
